When my son was born, at first I had many doubts and did not really know what to do or how to help him; we felt very bad and alone. In time we got to know the PRO-21 Foundation, and it was a very important support for our family. They guided us, explained how to stimulate him and, above all, taught us to trust his abilities. Little by little we have seen progress in his language and in the way he relates to others. As a mom I feel very grateful because the Foundation not only helps our children, it also helps us parents face things and know that we are not alone.
